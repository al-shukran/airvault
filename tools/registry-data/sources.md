## Rules

- Networks: mainnets only. Testnet names and deprecated chains are excluded. Logo slugs come from `RegistryIcons.kt`, the generated map of the shipped logos, and are matched to chains.json by normalised name, with `ALIASES` where the slug differs. Bitcoin is skipped as non-EVM. Chains in `exclusions.json` are not added, and `manual-networks.json` adds a chain that chainlist does not list as a mainnet.
- feeModel: `feemodel.json` decides. A chain listed there takes its value. Every other chain is `L1`. The rule and its evidence are in README.md.
- Tokens: a (chainId, contract) is accepted only if at least two of uniswap, superchain and coingecko list it with the same decimals. Symbols match case-insensitively. Decimals disagreement, single-source entries, two accepted contracts for one symbol and chain, or one contract under two symbols go to `out/tokens.unverified.json`.
- USDC: accepted only from Circle's official contract page (`circle-usdc-contract-addresses`). Bridged USDC (for example Binance-Peg on BNB Smart Chain) is not accepted.
- Gap tokens: a row in `gap-tokens.json` `accepted` is added only when its official source is listed there. Decimals are declared from that source and must match on chain (verify_onchain.py).
- Existing rows: names, symbols, icons and nativeSymbol values in the shipped JSON are kept. feeModel changes are listed below.
- Each token's icon is `token_<lowercase symbol>` if RegistryIcons.kt has that key, otherwise null.
