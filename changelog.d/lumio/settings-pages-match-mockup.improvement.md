Settings, Display, Transactions, Networks and Data Providers follow the approved design. Display has one Hide Balances switch for every amount, and a Theme row to follow the phone or keep Light or Dark. Transactions pauses 0 to 10 seconds before sending, 3 by default. Networks lists the EVM networks with a search field, and the Bitcoin servers moved to their own page below it. Data Providers groups the providers by what they serve.
