After a wallet is paired or restored, its accounts show at once with "Checking…" where each balance will be, and each balance appears as soon as its chain answers, instead of a page of placeholders until every chain has finished. A status line under the total counts the Bitcoin accounts checked so far, and then says when the balances were last updated. A chain that does not answer names itself under its accounts with a Retry that reads that chain again, and a Bitcoin balance from a scan that failed part way says Incomplete until a full scan completes.
